## Which data, and how we separate it

We only process data that you provide, or that is clearly provided to us when you provide it.

- **Directly identifying data**: data that leads directly to you or your household, such as name, address and e-mail address. We need it to invite and help you.
- **Other data**: measurements and characteristics of your home and heating system, linked to a **pseudonym** (a unique code) instead of your name.

We store both kinds of data separately; only the pseudonym connects them. We use that link only as long as needed, for example to answer your question to the helpdesk or to contact you if no measurements arrive for more than 24 hours.

## Data through the NeedForHeat app

You install the app from the Apple or Google app store, or as an installation file from {{ORGANISATION}}. We have no influence on what an app store does with your data; please read that provider's privacy statement.

After activation, the following data can be linked to your pseudonym:

1. **Measurement devices** you install in your home and connect through the app. For each device, the app shows what it measures. Examples:
   - *Smart meter module*: gas and electricity meter readings (every 5 to 60 minutes, depending on your smart meter);
   - *Living room module*: CO₂ concentration, temperature and relative humidity (every 10 minutes), and optionally the number of registered smartphones at home (via Bluetooth), to estimate ventilation;
   - *Boiler module*: temperatures and settings of your boiler and thermostat.
2. **Online data sources** you authorise in the app, for example the smart meter service of an independent service provider. We explicitly do *not* receive your user name and password for that service.
3. **Answers to questions in the app**, such as a rounded location for weather data and, if you fill them in, characteristics of your home.

Data collection stops at the end date of the campaign. If you want to stop earlier, please tell us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You can also stop it yourself by switching off the devices and revoking your consent at online data sources.

## Security

- Access to personal data is restricted to the helpdesk and a few designated staff.
- The app, devices and servers communicate only over encrypted connections (TLS) with servers in the {{DOMAIN}} domain.
- Directly identifying data is stored separately from other data; people who analyse measurements have no access to directly identifying data.
- At the latest {{RETENTION_PERIOD}} after the campaign ends, we delete the link between your pseudonym and your directly identifying data.

## Publishing data (optional)

*Delete this section if you do not publish data.*

After the link to your pseudonym has been deleted, we may publish the other data as open data, so it can support further research. Beforehand we check that the data cannot be traced to a home or person, for example by generalising locations and home characteristics. Parties that hold their own measurements of your home (such as a smart meter data provider) could compare those with the published data. We therefore wait {{WAITING_PERIOD}} after the campaign before publishing, so you can ask those parties to delete your data.
