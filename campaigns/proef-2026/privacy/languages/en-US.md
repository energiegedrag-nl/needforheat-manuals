# Privacy in this trial

version 20 November 2026

This trial is a technical test by Henri ter Hofte, as a private person, with only himself and a few acquaintances who ask for it themselves.

**What is collected.** Only the measurements of the devices you pair: for the living room module CO₂, temperature, humidity, the number of registered smartphones at home and the battery voltage; for the smart meter module the readings of your smart meter. The data are linked to an account number, not to your name. Who has which account number is only in an encrypted list kept by Henri.

**Where.** On a private server at Henri's home, in the Netherlands, reachable under energiegedrag.nl. App and devices send everything over encrypted connections. No data are passed on to others or published.

**How long.** Until the end of the trial; then the data are deleted. If you want your data removed earlier, just ask.

**Stopping.** Always possible, without giving a reason: unplug the device and let Henri know.

Questions: ask the person who invited you.
