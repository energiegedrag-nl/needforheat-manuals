# About this trial

This is a **trial measurement** by Henri ter Hofte, as a private person. Its purpose is to test whether the measurement devices, the NeedForHeat app and the server work well together before a real measurement campaign starts. Only Henri and a few acquaintances who ask for it themselves take part.

Questions about the trial: ask the person who invited you.

## About NeedForHeat

The NeedForHeat app and measurement system were originally developed by the [Research group Energy Transition](https://www.windesheim.nl/onderzoek/lectoraten/energietransitie) of [Windesheim University of Applied Sciences](https://www.windesheim.com/) and are open source. This trial uses its own, further developed version ([energiegedrag-nl on GitHub](https://github.com/energiegedrag-nl)). Windesheim is not involved in this trial.
