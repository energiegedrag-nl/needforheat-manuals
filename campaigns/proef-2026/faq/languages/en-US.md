# Frequently asked questions

### 1. Which measurement devices do I install?

The devices you received from Henri, usually the living room module and sometimes also the smart meter module. Scan the QR code on the device in the NeedForHeat app; the app helps you connect it to your Wi-Fi.

- [Living room module: installation](../../../../devices/twomes-co2-occupancy-scd41-m5coreink-firmware/installation/)
- [Smart meter module: installation](../../../../devices/twomes-p1-reader-firmware/installation/)

### 2. How do I pair a device again?

For example after a new router or when pairing did not work the first time. This differs per device; tap the question mark next to the device's name in the app, or see the frequently asked questions below.
