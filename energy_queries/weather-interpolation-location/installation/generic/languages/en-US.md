# Home location (weather zone)

You will soon be asked to provide the location of your home so the app can assign your residence to one of the 150 or so hexagonal weather zones in the Netherlands, each about 250 km². This weather zone is used to calculate the weather at your home using data from nearby KNMI weather stations.

Here are the three steps to complete this:

1. *Place the marker on your house on the map:* Move and zoom in on the map until the marker is on your house. You can also press the GPS button to go directly to your current location, which is usually your home.
2. *Confirm your weather zone:* A hexagonal zone is displayed. The app first shifts your location a little at random (about 10 kilometres on average), so your house is not always in the middle, and sometimes just in a neighbouring zone. That is intended.
3. *Send the weather zone:* Only the centre of the weather zone is sent to the server. Your exact location is not sent, for privacy reasons.
