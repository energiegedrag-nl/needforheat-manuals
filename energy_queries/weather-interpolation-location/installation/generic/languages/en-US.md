# Weather location

This question tells the study which weather belongs to your home: outdoor temperature, sun and wind. They are needed to learn from the measurements how your home loses heat.

## What you do

Choose your address or your current location and confirm. That is all; you only do this once.

## What happens to your location

- Your address and your exact location **do not leave your phone**.
- The app first shifts your location at random (about 10 kilometres on average) and then takes the centre of a hexagonal area of about 250 km² (the H3 grid). Only that centre goes to the research server.
- The area you see on the map afterwards is therefore usually not centred on your home. That is intended.
- The shift is drawn once and kept, so answering again does not give your home away after all.

The weather for that area comes from KNMI, the Dutch weather service. For the study an area of this size is accurate enough.

Questions? Ask the person who invited you to this study.
