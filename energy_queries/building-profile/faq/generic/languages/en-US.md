# Home address (heat signature) - FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

## 1. Why do I get this question?
The app asks for your home address so that it can then download data about your home from public registers. With it the app estimates the heat signature of your home. Your address is not sent to the server; only the rounded estimates are.

## 2. What is a heat signature?
Characteristics of the home itself: how much heat the outside lets through per degree of difference, how much heat the home can store (and so how fast it cools down), how much solar heat comes in (depending on the direction of your façades), how much air comes in through gaps when it is windy and how airtight the home is expected to be. It is not an energy label and says nothing about your energy use or how you heat.

## 3. Who sees my address?
Nobody in the study. The app asks for the public data itself, in such a way that the registers do not see which home it is about either: it asks for all addresses of the postcode, the buildings in an area of 300 by 300 metres, and the 3D data of your building together with that of seven other buildings nearby. Then the app picks your home on your phone. Only the results go to the server, rounded into a few coarse classes.

## 4. I live in an apartment. What now?
The calculation only works for single-family homes so far. The app says so and sends nothing; you can continue with the other steps.

## 5. Can I change my answer?
You cannot change a submitted answer. If you need to do so, see point 6.
